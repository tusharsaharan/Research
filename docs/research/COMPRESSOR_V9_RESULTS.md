Both highlighted candidates meet every cap. The character/clause candidate requires prefix enforcement on one example at 0.50; the strict hybrid on three at 0.33. Strict BM25 requires enforcement on 14 and 42 examples respectively. Preserve this information: a cap enforced by truncation is not a meaning-preservation guarantee, and the candidates use somewhat more of the allowed context than strict BM25.

The character/clause candidate's paired gain over strict BM25 at 0.50 is +0.83 percentage points, with an exploratory passage-bootstrap 95% interval of −7.50 to +9.17. The strict hybrid's gain at 0.33 is +3.33 points, interval −5.83 to +12.50. Neither establishes superiority. Against strict LLMLingua-2 large, the 0.50 character/clause gain is +11.67 points, interval +2.50 to +20.83, before accounting for adaptive method selection and multiple comparisons.

The clause approach is fragile at higher compression: the same character/clause method scores only 42.50% at 0.33. Do not silently select a different winner for every dataset. English results also matter: at 0.50 this candidate scores 80.83% versus strict BM25's 80.00%, while uncompressed is 91.67%.

Three inspected cases illustrate why answer accuracy alone needs care:

- `bb-30`: the candidate answers correctly after selecting an endurance-related span, but BM25 already retains the explicit endurance requirement. This is not clear evidence that BM25 lost the answer; answer-model behavior contributes.
- `bb-387`: an apparent strict-hybrid win uses a passage with a translation error. English says “succeeding Henry Kissinger”; Hinglish says “Henry Kissinger ko serve kiya.” The English option “Carter” also becomes “Driver.” The candidate and baseline retain the same faulty sentence. Flag for bilingual verification; do not repair/remove it selectively after scoring.
- `bb-418`: the character/clause candidate drops the petition/signature evidence and selects the church's opposition to marriage recognition; it answers church instead of protesters. This is a concrete evidence-ranking failure.

## Transfer protocol

Freeze **both** `bhybrid_strict` and `bhybrid_char_clause` at **both** rates for every dataset. Compare with original, strict BM25 and strict LLMLingua-2 large using the same answer model, tokenizer, prompt policy and cap enforcement. No compressor parameter changes after v9 selection. Selection remains explicitly post-v9 development selection.

The available document-QA suite comprises Belebele, HotpotQA, MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, SQuAD and MeetingBank. The next two versioned jobs cover all six, selecting up to 100 distinct passages per dataset after excluding normalized exact contexts from all completed compressor pilots. The prepared manifests record actual counts and exclusion-input hashes. Only Belebele currently supplies paired Hinglish; the other tasks test English transfer, not Hinglish generalization.

This check reduces direct pilot overlap and tests transfer, but cannot establish absence of overfitting: historical dataset exposure and near-duplicate source overlap remain. A final paper claim still needs separately frozen, newly held-out source families and linked bilingual verification. Report every dataset/rate, including regressions; do not tune on this transfer batch and then call the same batch a test set. Other router/instruction benchmarks are not interchangeable with this passage-compression QA protocol.
